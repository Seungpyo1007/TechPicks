One app, two native languages.

Colour

The palette now comes out of the logo itself. Sampled from the mark: the pills are #0C78D8 falling to #0B5490 and lifting to #0090F0, over a graphite #484848. That blue is the primary on both platforms.

The red is gone. Nothing in the interface competes with the mark any more.

iOS — Liquid Glass

Content is the only opaque layer. Chrome is glass: header and the floating tab capsule blur and saturate whatever scrolls beneath them, lit by a specular top edge.

Concentric radii — 28 on cards, 22 inside them, full round on controls. SF type, large titles, tinted glyphs.

Android — Material 3

Tonal, not translucent. Surfaces step through surface → container → container-high; the primary container carries emphasis instead of blur.

Large top app bar, 80dp navigation bar with the pill active-indicator, extended FAB, Roboto, M3 shape scale.

What carried over

The product thinking: a shortlist that persists, one composite index shown with its parts, compare as a tab, rankings back in-app instead of a webview, and Scan / 3D wired to real intent.

What changed

Every visual. Flat red Swiss grid out; glass and tonal Material in. Two chromes, one content model — the same screens read native on each platform without forking the product.

Next

Dark mode for both (the glass wants it), the add-to-shortlist transition, and real product photography — drag images onto the slot on the detail screen and they persist. v1 is still in the project if you want to compare.
